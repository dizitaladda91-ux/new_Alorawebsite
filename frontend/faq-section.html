What are the 5 basics of a skincare routine?

A complete everyday skincare routine consists of: 1. Cleanser (to remove dirt & pollution), 2. Toner (to balance pH), 3. Serum (concentrated actives targeting specific concerns), 4. Moisturizer/Cream (to lock in hydration), and 5. Sunscreen/SPF (to shield against UV rays and premature ageing).

What is the difference between a face serum and a toner?

A toner re-balances your skin's natural pH and preps pores after cleansing. A face serum is a high-potency treatment with smaller molecules that penetrate deeper into the dermis to deliver active botanicals like saffron and hyaluronic acid directly to skin cells.
